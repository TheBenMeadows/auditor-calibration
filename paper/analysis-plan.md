# P1 analysis plan

Written 2026-10-17 before the DeepSeek Flash lane finished and before the Fable 5.1 lane started. Nothing below may be changed after those results exist without recording the change and the reason in this file's history.

## Data

Case set: `cases-paper-144.json`, 144 cases (52 real findings, 92 false claims) from four sources: 59 original, 52 mutated, 19 council (non-Squint), 14 Squint council. Opus 5.5 ran the 130 non-Squint cases as variant `baseline` and the 14 Squint cases as `v3`; the two are pooled into one 144-case Opus lane. The other lanes ran all 144: SWE-2 High `v1`, DeepSeek Flash `v2`, Sonnet 5.5 `v4`, Haiku 5.5 `v5` (three runs per case each) and Fable 5.1 `v6` (one run per case).

A run row is one (case, rep) in a variant's `results.jsonl`. Failed attempts sit in `errors.jsonl` and are not rows.

## Failures

Each lane reports attempted runs, completed rows and failed attempts. Failures are classed as infrastructure (the provider or tooling failed: `unrecognized_model` from an empty provider balance, the `judge-run` health-file race) or model (the model's own output was unusable: malformed JSON, reaching for a tool the lane forbids). Infrastructure failures are re-run until they complete; model failures are kept as results and reported as a per-lane format-failure rate. A case with no completed row in a lane is missing from that lane and from every paired comparison that includes that lane.

## Primary metrics, per lane, case level

The unit of analysis is the case. A case's p_true is the mean of its completed runs' p_true; its correctness is the mean over completed runs of (p_true ≥ 0.5) == label. The 0.5 threshold is fixed by the harness and is not tuned.

- recall = sum of case correctness over real findings / number of real findings, with a Wilson 95% interval
- specificity = the same over false claims
- AUROC of case p_true, real findings against false claims
- Brier score = mean over cases of the mean over runs of (p_true − label)²
- for lanes with three runs: the share of cases right on at least one run and on every run, overall and per class

These match `scripts/summarize.mjs`. Every headline number is recomputed by a second, independent script from the raw rows (it derives correctness and Brier from p_true and the label, not from the stored `grade` fields), and the two must agree exactly.

## Per-source table

Recall and specificity per lane per source (original, mutated, council, Squint). The Squint cases are all false claims, so only specificity applies to them.

## Comparisons between lanes

Superseded on 2026-10-17 and 2026-10-18 by the Changes entries below (claim-family bootstrap in place of DeLong, family-level sign-flips, per-case single-run comparisons for one-run lanes); the text of this section is kept as first written, for the record.

All pairs of the six lanes, on the cases both lanes completed.

- Accuracy: per case, the difference in case correctness between the two lanes; a paired sign-flip randomization test on the mean difference, 20,000 random sign assignments, fixed seed 20261017, two-sided.
- Recall and specificity separately: the same test restricted to real findings and to false claims.
- AUROC: DeLong's test for two correlated AUROCs on the shared cases.

Holm correction within each family (accuracy, recall, specificity, AUROC) across all lane pairs. Fable has one run per case, so its case correctness is 0 or 1; that is stated next to every comparison that includes it.

## Disagreements

A disagreement is a case whose majority verdict in a lane (more than half its completed runs) contradicts the label. Every disagreement trace is read. Each is classed as model error, harness or tool problem, ambiguous claim, or suspected label error. A suspected label error goes to the maintainer; no label is changed by the analysis.

## Changes

- 2026-10-17, before any DeepSeek re-run or Fable result: a case's p_true is the exact mean of the reported decimals (Python: `Fraction(Decimal(repr(x)))`; `summarize.mjs`: the float mean rounded to 1e-9). A plain binary float sum left some mathematically equal three-run means a last bit apart, so AUROC ties were decided by summation order; the two scripts disagreed by up to 0.0007 (Haiku 0.966137 against 0.966869) and neither was right for every lane. Exact values on the five complete lanes: Opus 130-case 0.996055 (no ties), SWE-2 0.952550, DeepSeek (partial) 0.981119, Sonnet 0.982023, Haiku 0.966869.
